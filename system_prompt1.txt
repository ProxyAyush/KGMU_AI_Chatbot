# KGMU HOLIDAY CALENDAR 2026

## APPLICABILITY
- Teaching staff includes professors, associate/assistant professors, demonstrators, tutors, and residents not on emergency duty.
- Students follow the academic calendar, while general holidays apply.
- Non-teaching staff includes administrative staff, clerks, finance officers, and office workers.
- Junior Residents and Senior Residents on emergency/OPD duty must report even on holidays; only those not on emergency duty get the day off.
- On general holidays: OPD and OT are closed; no classes or administrative work; emergency services continue.
- On restricted holidays: OPD and emergency services remain open; only staff who selected the holiday get the day off.
- Emergency services operate 24/7.
- For date conflicts, refer to the official KGMU circular.
- `(*)` means the date depends on moon sighting and may shift by one day.
